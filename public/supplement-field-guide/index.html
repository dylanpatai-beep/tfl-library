Start here

Supplements aren't magic, and they aren't one-size-fits-all. Most people are sold a stack by whoever has an affiliate link. This guide does the opposite: it breaks the whole landscape into plain categories, tells you how strong the actual evidence is, and lets you build a personal shortlist you can take to bloodwork and a doctor. Food first, test before you guess, and add one thing at a time.

This is education, not medical advice. The Fight Life isn't your doctor, and this guide doesn't prescribe anything. Evidence ratings reflect published peer-reviewed research (systematic reviews and meta-analyses) and can shift as new studies land. Dose ranges are general adult reference ranges, shown only for supplements you've tagged — they are not personalized guidance. Remember: more is not better, and "natural" does not mean "safe." Before starting anything, talk to a qualified professional and get bloodwork — especially if you're pregnant, nursing, under 18, on medication, or managing a health condition. Competing athletes: check every ingredient against your sport's banned list (WADA / USADA), because a few entries here are prohibited in competition.
