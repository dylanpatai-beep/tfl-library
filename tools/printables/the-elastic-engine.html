Gym cheat sheet

The Elastic Engine2-Day Striker S&C Program

Rules
  • Explosive work first, while you're fresh. Never train power to failure.
  • Move every rep with max intent, as fast as possible.
  • Low reps, full recovery between explosive sets.
  • 2 non-consecutive days a week, 48–72 h between hard explosive sessions.
  • Taper volume in the final 7–10 days before a fight.
Day A
Linear power & posterior chain
  1. Primer: 90/90 breathing + hip & shoulder CARs3–4 min · set the canister
  2. Box jump (step down, reset each rep)4×3 · land soft, absorb
  3. Med-ball overhead slam4×4 · full-body extension → flexion power
  4. Trap-bar or conventional deadlift4×3 (heavy, fast off the floor) · braced, ground-up
  5. Push-press or landmine press3×5 · leg-driven overhead power
  6. Weighted pull-up5×3
  7. Ab-wheel rollout or hollow-body hold3×6–8 / 20–30 s · resist the spine extending
  8. Suitcase carry (heavy, one side)3×30 m/side · resist side-bend
  9. Med-ball rotational throw (side-on to wall)3×5/side · rear hip → opposite lat → release
Option: Reverse hyper (Westside), if you have access. Sub in for or add to the deadlift on lighter weeks.
Day B
Rotational & lateral power
  1. Primer: 90/90 breathing + 90/90 hip switches + open books3–4 min · prep rotation
  2. Broad jump (stick the landing)4×3 · deceleration control
  3. Lateral bound (skater)3×4/side · hip stability on landing
  4. Front squat or heavy goblet squat4×4 (braced, fast up) · vertical, pressurized torso
  5. Single-leg RDL3×6/side · lat ↔ opposite glute + balance
  6. Weighted dip5×3
  7. Pallof press (cable or band)3×8/side · resist rotation
  8. McGill Big 3: curl-up · side plank · bird dogdescending pyramid (e.g., 6/4/2 "reps," ~10 s holds)
  9. Cable/band horizontal chop + lift3×6/side each · high-to-low and low-to-high
Daily (any day)
McGill Big 3 + mobility can run every day.
