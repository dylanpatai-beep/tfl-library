## Why it's here

Flanken ribs cook in under ten minutes because they're cut thin across the bone. Marinate the night before a training day and dinner is done faster than a delivery order — with macros you actually control.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb flanken-cut beef short ribs (about 1/4" thick)
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 Asian pear, grated (this is the tenderizer — don't skip it)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 1 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Whisk everything except the ribs into a marinade.
2. Submerge the ribs. Overnight in the fridge is right; 4 hours is the minimum.
3. Grill over high heat, 3–4 minutes per side. You want char on the edges.
4. Rest 5 minutes, then cut between the bones. Finish with green onion.

## Notes

- Serve over rice with cucumber kimchi if it's a hard-training day; over salad greens on a cut.
- The marinade freezes with the raw ribs — bag it flat and it's a future no-effort dinner.
