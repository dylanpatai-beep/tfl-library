**Yield:** 8 slices · 9x5 loaf pan · 350°F · 40–45 min bake

*About 10 minutes to mix and 40–45 minutes in the oven. Let it cool fully, then chill the frosting for 30+ minutes to set.*

## Ingredients (makes 8 slices)

**Bread**

- 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
- 1 ripe banana, mashed
- 3 eggs + 1/2 cup liquid egg whites
- 1/2 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- 3 Tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup oat flour
- 2 scoops chocolate casein
- 2 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa (optional)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- Pinch of salt

**Frosting**

- 3/4 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1/4 scoop chocolate casein
- 1 tsp honey
- Cinnamon, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5 loaf pan.
2. Whisk the pumpkin, banana, eggs, egg whites, yogurt, honey and vanilla.
3. Fold in the oat flour, casein, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, spice and salt. Don't overmix.
4. Bake 40–45 minutes. Pull it when a toothpick shows a few moist crumbs (a little early = fudgier).
5. Cool fully. Stir the frosting together, spread it on, and chill 30+ minutes to set. Cut 8 slices.

## Macros (approx., varies by brand)

- Per slice (with frosting): about 205 calories, 17 g protein, 27 g carbs, 4 g fat
- Whole loaf: about 1,620 calories and 136 g protein
- Assumes chocolate casein at about 120 cal / 24 g protein per scoop, and includes the cocoa
