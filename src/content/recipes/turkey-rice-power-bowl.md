**Yield:** Serves 1

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup cooked white rice
- About 1/2 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- Salt, pepper and your favorite seasoning
- About 1/2 cup sliced carrots (orange and purple)
- About 1/3 cup sauerkraut
- About 1/2 avocado, sliced
- About 2 Tbsp chopped pepperoncini
- About 1 Tbsp honey mustard

## Method

1. Cook the rice, or reheat leftover rice.
2. Brown the turkey in a pan over medium-high heat, breaking it up, and season with salt, pepper and your seasoning.
3. Slice the carrots thin.
4. Build the bowl: rice, turkey, carrots, sauerkraut, avocado and pepperoncini.
5. Drizzle the honey mustard over the top.
