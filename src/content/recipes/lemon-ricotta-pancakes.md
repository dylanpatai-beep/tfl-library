## Why it's here

Rest days need a breakfast worth waking up for. These hold real protein without protein-powder chalkiness, and the batter comes together in one bowl.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole-milk ricotta
- 2 eggs
- 3/4 cup flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Zest of one lemon + juice of half
- Pinch of salt
- Butter for the pan

## Method

1. Whisk ricotta, eggs, lemon zest, and juice until smooth.
2. Fold in flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Don't overmix — lumps are fine.
3. Medium heat, butter the pan, 1/4-cup scoops. Flip when the edges set, about 2–3 minutes a side.
4. Stack, more lemon zest on top, honey or maple if the day's earned it.

## Notes

- Batter keeps covered in the fridge for a day — make it the night before a slow morning.
