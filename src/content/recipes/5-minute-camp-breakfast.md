**Yield:** Serves 1

> **Note:** "5-minute" assumes the microwave route for the sweet potato. Baking it takes 45–50 minutes but tastes better.

## Ingredients

- 3 whole eggs
- 1/2 cup liquid egg whites
- Hot sauce, to taste
- 1 medium sweet potato
- 1 Tbsp apple butter
- 1 cup frozen blueberries
- 1 tsp honey
- Ceylon cinnamon, to taste
- Optional: 1/2 cup whole kefir on the side

## Method

1. Poke the sweet potato and microwave 5–7 minutes, flipping halfway (or bake at 400°F for 45–50 minutes for better flavor).
2. Fry the eggs and whites in a nonstick pan, then add hot sauce.
3. Slice the sweet potato and top with apple butter.
4. Top the frozen blueberries with honey and cinnamon.
5. Optional: 1/2 cup kefir on the side for gut health.

## Macros (approx., varies by brand)

- About 590 calories and 34 g protein without kefir
- About 670 calories and 39 g protein with kefir
