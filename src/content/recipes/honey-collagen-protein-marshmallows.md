**Yield:** 16 squares · 8x8 pan · set 2–4 hrs

*About 25 minutes of hands-on time, then 2–4 hours to set.*

## Ingredients (makes 16 squares)

- 3 Tbsp unflavored gelatin (3 packets), bloomed in 1/2 cup cold water
- 2 scoops unflavored collagen peptides
- 1/3 cup honey + 1/4 cup water
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Sprinkle the gelatin over 1/2 cup cold water. Let it sit 5 minutes.
2. Warm the honey and 1/4 cup water to about 160–180°F, not boiling. Take it off the heat.
3. Pour it over the gelatin and stir to dissolve. Add the collagen, vanilla and salt.
4. Beat with a hand mixer on high for 8–10 minutes, until thick, white and fluffy.
5. Spread into a parchment-lined 8x8 pan. Set 2–4 hours. Cut with an oiled knife.

**Note:** Collagen adds protein but doesn't gel. The gelatin does the setting.

## Macros (approx., varies by brand)

- Per square: about 35 calories, 3.4 g protein, 5.8 g carbs, 0 g fat
- Whole batch: about 550 calories, 54 g protein, 92 g carbs
- Assumes gelatin at about 23 cal / 6 g protein per 7 g packet, collagen at about 70 cal / 18 g protein per 20 g scoop, and honey at about 64 cal / 17 g carbs per Tbsp
