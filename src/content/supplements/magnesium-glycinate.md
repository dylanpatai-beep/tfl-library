## Mechanism

Cofactor in 300+ enzymatic reactions — muscle contraction, nervous-system regulation, energy production. Glycinate chelation improves absorption and adds glycine, which has mild sleep-supporting evidence of its own.

## What it's for

Sleep depth, next-day soreness, and covering the gap: hard training and sweating burn through magnesium, and most training diets run short. This won't fix a broken schedule — it tops up a real deficit.

## Food first

Pumpkin seeds, almonds, spinach, dark chocolate, black beans. A real food-first pass covers a lot of this — the supplement is for what training volume leaves uncovered.

## How I take it

- 300–400 mg elemental magnesium as glycinate, about an hour before bed.
- Paired with a wind-down, not as a substitute for one.

## Watch-outs & interactions

- Skip magnesium oxide — it's cheap because you absorb almost none of it.
- Doses well above 400 mg elemental can loosen digestion even as glycinate.
- If shift-work sleep is the real problem, fix light and caffeine timing first. The supplement is the last 10%.

## For the fighter

Heavy sweat rates in camp deplete magnesium faster than a normal training diet replaces it. If cramping or poor sleep shows up mid-camp, this plus sodium/potassium is the first check — before anything exotic.
