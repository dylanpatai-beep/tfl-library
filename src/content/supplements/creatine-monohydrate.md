## Mechanism

Increases phosphocreatine stores in skeletal muscle and brain, buffering ATP regeneration during high-intensity work. Emerging evidence for neurological benefit — working memory, fatigue resistance, possibly protective around head impact.

## What it's for

Strength, power output, lean mass, muscular endurance, and cognitive performance. The most researched ergogenic aid in existence — hundreds of trials, decades of use, and the effect shows up in the room: better repeat power output in sparring rounds and heavier top sets.

## Food first

Red meat (~1–2 g per pound), herring, salmon. If you don't eat meat, your stores are essentially depleted — supplementation matters more, not less.

## How I take it

- 3–5 g of plain monohydrate, mixed into whatever liquid is nearby.
- No loading phase — daily dosing saturates stores in about 3–4 weeks.
- Taken every day including rest days. Timing genuinely does not matter.

## Watch-outs & interactions

- Buy plain monohydrate (Creapure if you want third-party surety). Every fancier form costs more and tests worse or equal.
- Expect a pound or two of intramuscular water — plan around it if you're cutting to a number.
- The kidney-damage myth has been refuted repeatedly in healthy populations.

## For the fighter

Relevant across nearly every combat sport — phosphocreatine-driven explosive output is what striking, clinch work, and short grappling exchanges run on. The cognitive angle matters double if you take head contact or work 24-hour shifts.
