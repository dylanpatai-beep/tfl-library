## Why

Knees that only train partial ranges fail in the ranges fights happen in — deep steps, checked kicks, scrambles. Loading the knee through its full range, patiently, is what makes it stop complaining.

## Setup

1. Long split stance, front foot flat (heel down or elevated on a slant board).
2. Lower until the back knee approaches the floor and the front knee travels well past the toes — hamstring covers calf at the bottom.
3. Torso tall. The rear hip should feel a stretch; the front knee should feel work, not pain.

## The work

- 3 sets of 8–10 per leg, slow on the way down (3 seconds).
- Bodyweight until the full range is owned. Then a single dumbbell held at the chest.
- Front heel stays down. If it lifts, shorten the stance before adding load.

## Progression

Bodyweight full-range → goblet load → rear foot elevated → slant board under the front foot. Earn each step over weeks. Sharp pain is a stop sign; the stretch-work ache is the point.
