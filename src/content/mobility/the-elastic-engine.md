## Rules

- Explosive work first, while you're fresh. Never train power to failure.
- Move every rep with max intent, as fast as possible.
- Low reps, full recovery between explosive sets.
- 2 non-consecutive days a week, 48–72 h between hard explosive sessions.
- Taper volume in the final 7–10 days before a fight.

## Day A — Linear power & posterior chain

1. **Primer:** 90/90 breathing + hip & shoulder CARs — 3–4 min · *set the canister*
2. **Box jump** (step down, reset each rep) — 4×3 · *land soft, absorb*
3. **Med-ball overhead slam** — 4×4 · *full-body extension → flexion power*
4. **Trap-bar or conventional deadlift** — 4×3 (heavy, fast off the floor) · *braced, ground-up*
5. **Push-press or landmine press** — 3×5 · *leg-driven overhead power*
6. **Weighted pull-up** — 5×3
7. **Ab-wheel rollout or hollow-body hold** — 3×6–8 / 20–30 s · *resist the spine extending*
8. **Suitcase carry** (heavy, one side) — 3×30 m/side · *resist side-bend*
9. **Med-ball rotational throw** (side-on to wall) — 3×5/side · *rear hip → opposite lat → release*

**Option:** Reverse hyper (Westside), if you have access. Sub in for or add to the deadlift on lighter weeks.

## Day B — Rotational & lateral power

1. **Primer:** 90/90 breathing + 90/90 hip switches + open books — 3–4 min · *prep rotation*
2. **Broad jump** (stick the landing) — 4×3 · *deceleration control*
3. **Lateral bound** (skater) — 3×4/side · *hip stability on landing*
4. **Front squat or heavy goblet squat** — 4×4 (braced, fast up) · *vertical, pressurized torso*
5. **Single-leg RDL** — 3×6/side · *lat ↔ opposite glute + balance*
6. **Weighted dip** — 5×3
7. **Pallof press** (cable or band) — 3×8/side · *resist rotation*
8. **McGill Big 3:** curl-up · side plank · bird dog — descending pyramid (e.g., 6/4/2 "reps," ~10 s holds)
9. **Cable/band horizontal chop + lift** — 3×6/side each · *high-to-low and low-to-high*

## Daily (any day)

McGill Big 3 + mobility can run every day.
