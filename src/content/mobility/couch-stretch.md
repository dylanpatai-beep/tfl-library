## Why

Tight hip flexors steal kick height, shorten your stride, and feed low-back ache after long sits — a firehouse shift or a desk day does the same damage. This is the highest-return two minutes in the hip toolbox.

## Setup

1. Kneel facing away from a wall or couch. Back shin goes up the wall, knee in the corner where floor meets wall.
2. Front foot steps out into a lunge.
3. Squeeze the glute of the wall-side leg *before* you raise your torso. That squeeze is the exercise.

## The work

- 2 minutes per side. Breathe slow — if you're shaking or holding breath, back the torso down.
- Progress by walking the torso more upright over weeks, not by forcing it day one.

## Progression

Once vertical torso is comfortable: add a reach overhead on the wall-side arm, or move to weighted hip-flexor work (rear-foot-elevated split squats carry the pattern into strength).
